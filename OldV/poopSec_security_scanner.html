poopSec
Web Security Scanner · Made with chaos & caffeine

so yeah, poopSec is a free, no-nonsense web security scanner that we built because most security tools out there are either too expensive, too complicated, or just straight up boring. we wanted something that actually tells you what's wrong in plain english (well, informal english) — and helps you fix it without making you feel like an idiot.

it checks all the important security headers, looks for stuff you accidentally left exposed (like your server version, powered-by headers, etc.), validates your HTTPS setup, and gives you a proper grade with detailed recommendations. think of it like a brutally honest friend who happens to know a lot about web security.

all scans happen in your browser — we don't store your URLs, we don't log your data, we don't sell your info to sketchy third parties. we're not that kind of people.
